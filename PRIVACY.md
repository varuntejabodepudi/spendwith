# Track-Spense Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Track-Spense ("the app") is a personal finance tracking application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Who is responsible

Track-Spense is maintained by the Track-Spense project. For privacy questions or requests, contact the maintainer through the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/vtcxxager/spendwith) by opening an issue. Do not include account numbers, transaction details, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

## 2. Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, information you may enter or create in the app can include:

- Account labels, bank names, account nicknames, and account types.
- Transaction amounts, dates, descriptions, categories, and notes.
- Budgets, preferences, and other app settings.

Do not enter full bank account numbers, card numbers, CVV values, PINs, passwords, or one-time passcodes into the app.

## 3. Where information is stored

Track-Spense is designed around local storage. Financial records created in the current app are stored in the app's private storage on your Android device. The app is not designed to send those records to a Track-Spense-operated cloud database.

Information may leave the app's private storage if you choose to export or share a file, create a device backup, use Android backup features, or otherwise share information using your device. Those copies are governed by the destination, service, and settings you choose.

Uninstalling the app normally removes its private local data. Exported files and copies stored elsewhere are not automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

## 4. Permissions and optional device features

Android may ask for permissions when a feature needs access to a device capability. Granting a permission is optional unless Android or a particular feature clearly indicates otherwise. You can review or revoke permissions in Android Settings.

If a version of the app offers transaction detection from SMS or notifications, that feature should be used only after you enable it and grant the relevant Android permission. Such information is intended to support transaction detection on your device; do not assume that granting a permission connects your bank account or authorizes access to your bank.

The exact permissions and feature availability can vary by app version and Android version. Review the permission prompts shown by your installed version.

## 5. Sharing and disclosure

Track-Spense does not sell your personal financial records. The app is not designed to disclose transaction or account records to the Track-Spense maintainer.

You control whether to export, share, or otherwise disclose information from your device. Android, your device manufacturer, backup provider, or a third-party app you choose to use may process information under their own privacy terms.

## 6. Data security

The app stores its records in Android app storage and relies on the security controls provided by Android and your device. Keep your device protected with a secure screen lock, install operating-system updates, and avoid sharing exported financial files publicly.

No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free. You are responsible for protecting your device and any exported copies.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you delete it within the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior and any copies you have exported or shared. You can delete exported copies separately using the app or location where they are stored.

## 8. Children's privacy

Track-Spense is a general personal finance utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the project through a support channel, contact the maintainer using the GitHub repository.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page. The "Last updated" date above indicates when the policy was most recently revised. Material changes should also be reflected in the app's store disclosures where required.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the [Track-Spense GitHub repository](https://github.com/vtcxxager/spendwith). Please avoid posting personal or financial information publicly.

---

This policy describes the intended local-first behavior of the current Track-Spense project. The published Google Play Data safety answers and any in-app disclosures must be checked against the exact release build before publication.
