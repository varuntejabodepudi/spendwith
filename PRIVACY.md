## Your money is personal. Your data should be, too.

SpendWith is a personal finance and expense-tracking application designed around local storage on your Android device. This policy explains what information may be handled by the app, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

> **In short:** Financial records you create in SpendWith are intended to remain in the app's private storage on your device. The app is not designed to send those records to a SpendWith-operated cloud database. Features and permissions can vary by app version, so please read the details below.

## 1. Who is responsible

SpendWith is maintained as an independent personal finance app project.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the [SpendWith GitHub repository](https://github.com/vtcxxager/spendwith). Please do not post private financial information, account identifiers, transaction details, passwords, or screenshots containing sensitive data in a public issue.

## 2. Information the app handles

Depending on the features available in the version you use and the information you choose to provide, SpendWith may handle:

| Information | Examples | Why it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account details | Bank name, account nickname, account type, last four digits if entered | To organise your accounts |
| Transaction details | Amount, date, description, category, transaction type | To record and display your financial activity |
| App preferences | Theme and other settings | To remember your choices |
| Optional bill details | Bill name, amount and due date | To help you track upcoming payments |

SpendWith does not need your full bank account number, card number, CVV, PIN, banking password, or one-time passcode for ordinary manual expense tracking. Do not enter these sensitive credentials into the app.

## 3. Where your information is stored

SpendWith is designed with a **local-first storage model**. Financial records created in the current app are stored in the app's private storage on your Android device. The app is not designed to transmit those financial records to a SpendWith-operated cloud database.

Information can leave the app's private storage if you choose to:

- Export or share a file from the app.
- Use Android device backup or restore features.
- Share information with another app or service using Android's sharing tools.

Any exported or shared copy is handled according to the destination app, service, and settings you select.

Uninstalling SpendWith normally removes its private app data from the device. It does not automatically delete files you previously exported, shared, or stored elsewhere. Android backup behaviour may also affect what is retained.

## 4. Permissions and optional features

SpendWith requests Android permissions only when a feature needs them. You can review or revoke app permissions in your device's Android Settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not work.

### SMS and transaction detection

If SMS-based transaction detection is available and enabled in your version of SpendWith, the app may request the relevant SMS permission to identify financial transaction alerts and help organise transaction information.

- SMS access is intended for the app's financial-management functionality.
- Transaction detection must be used only after the required permission and user consent are provided.
- The intended design is to process relevant information on your device, not upload your SMS inbox to a SpendWith-operated server.
- Receiving a bank SMS does not mean SpendWith has connected to your bank account or can initiate payments.
- OTPs, promotional messages, and unrelated messages should not be treated as financial transactions.

The exact permissions and behaviour depend on the installed app version and Android version. Always review the permission explanation shown in the app and the Android system prompt.

### Notifications

If you enable notifications, SpendWith may display transaction-related alerts, reminders, or financial summaries generated from information available to the app. You can manage notification permissions and settings through Android.

## 5. Sharing and disclosure

SpendWith does not sell your personal financial records.

The app is not designed to disclose your account or transaction records to the SpendWith maintainer. You decide whether to export or share information from your device.

Android, your device manufacturer, backup provider, and third-party apps or services that you choose to use may process information under their own privacy policies and settings.

## 6. Security

SpendWith stores its records in Android app storage and relies on the security protections provided by Android and your device.

You can help protect your information by:

- Using a secure screen lock.
- Keeping Android and the app up to date.
- Not sharing your device unlock credentials.
- Keeping exported financial files in a secure location.
- Avoiding the inclusion of sensitive financial information in public support requests.

No electronic storage or transfer method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Your app records remain in the app's local storage until you delete them using available app controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behaviour.

Exported files and copies shared with other apps or services must be deleted separately from those destinations.

## 8. Children's privacy

SpendWith is a general personal finance utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the project through a support channel, contact the maintainer using the repository link below. Do not include the child's sensitive information in a public issue.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, permissions, or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL, and the revision date at the top will be updated.

Any applicable Google Play disclosures must accurately reflect the data practices and permissions of the version distributed through Google Play.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or policy-related requests, visit:

**[SpendWith GitHub repository](https://github.com/vtcxxager/spendwith)**

Please use a private communication channel if you need to discuss anything sensitive. Do not publish account numbers, transaction records, OTPs, passwords, or other personal financial information in public issues.
